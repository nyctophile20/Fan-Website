ABOUT THE ARTIST

Darshan Raval

Darshan Raval is an Indian singer, songwriter and composer known for his soulful voice and emotionally driven music.

From romantic melodies to energetic Bollywood tracks, his music has created a strong connection with listeners across generations.

His journey includes independent music as well as playback singing, with songs spanning Hindi and Gujarati music.
